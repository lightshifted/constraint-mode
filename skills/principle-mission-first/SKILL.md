# Mission first

A constraint is only defined relative to a goal. Without one, "bottleneck" just means "the thing that annoys us."

**When:** Any talk of constraints, priorities, focus, bottlenecks, or what to work on.

**Do:**
- Lock a one-line mission that names what increases and by roughly how much.
- Missing, ask once. Do not infer a mission from the backlog, the repo, or the tone of the request.
- A provisional mission is allowed only when the owner says so. Label it `guess` and carry that label into every claim built on it.

**Refuse:**
- Debating the constraint with no goal in scope.
- Accepting an aspiration as a mission. "Grow", "win", "be great" name a mood. A mission names a quantity and a direction.
- Two missions at once. Ask which one this period serves.

**Test:** Delete the mission sentence. Does "the constraint" still pick out something specific? If no, you do not have a mission yet.
