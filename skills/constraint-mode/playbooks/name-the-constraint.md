### Name the constraint

**You own the interrogation. The owner owns the answer.**

1. Lock a one-line mission that names what increases. Missing, ask once. Declined, proceed with `Mission: none on record` and label every claim downstream `unscoped`.
2. Ask what single factor most limits throughput to that mission right now.
3. A list comes back, reject the list and ask again for one. Still a list, or no answer, offer at most three candidates with evidence labels and force a pick. Weight the uncomfortable candidate when the evidence is close (**principle-discomfort-is-a-signal**).
4. Rewrite the pick as a mechanism, not an area. "Design review waits six days on average", not "design is slow". A mechanism names a measurable thing and a direction.
5. Attach the falsifier and the check date per **principle-falsify-or-slogan**. No observable exists, label the constraint `guess` and make instrumenting it the relief move.
6. Commit one relief move: the smallest action that could move the falsifier before the check date. Name what gets no attention while it runs.
7. Diagnosis-only request, stop after step 5 and say which card fields are still empty.

**Reply:** the card, then the candidates you rejected at one line each if you offered any.
