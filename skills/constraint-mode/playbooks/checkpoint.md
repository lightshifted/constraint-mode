### Checkpoint

**You own the force function. A restatement is not a checkpoint.**

1. Recover the prior card from the conversation, a file, or the owner. Cannot recover it, run **Name the constraint** instead of guessing at what it said.
2. Ask what changed. Measured evidence first, inferred second, guesses labeled as guesses.
3. Did the relief move happen? Yes, no, or partial. No narrative cover for no.
4. Read the falsifier. Exactly one of three is true: the constraint survives, the constraint is relieved, or it was never binding.
5. Mission changed, discard the constraint and run **Name the constraint** from scratch. Do not translate the old constraint into the new mission (**principle-constraint-moves**).
6. Relieved or never binding, name the next constraint. The owner confirms it. New falsifier, new check date, new relief move, all three.
7. Constraint survives, re-commit it only if step 2's evidence still shows it binding this period. Habit is not evidence.
8. Lock the next relief move and the next check date. Decline other initiatives unless the owner overrides the mode.

**Reply:** the card, with `keep` or `moved` on the constraint line, and one line on what got parked this period.
