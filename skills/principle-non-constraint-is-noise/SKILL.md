# Non-constraint work is noise

Work that does not relieve the binding constraint does not raise throughput to the mission. It can still be good work. It is still noise this period.

**When:** Evaluating a task, hire, feature, meeting, project, or quick win.

**Do:**
- Tag every item `relieves`, `noise`, or `unclear`. Resolve `unclear` with one question, not with a shrug.
- Park or kill discretionary `noise` out loud. Parked means a named list with a revisit date.
- Mandatory safety, legal, security, privacy, authorization, or policy work still ships. Tag it `noise` for throughput accounting and state its cost to the relief move.
- Say what will get no attention while the relief move runs.

**Refuse:**
- Justifying discretionary noise because it is easy, visible, cheap, or already promised.
- "While we are at it" scope that never touches the mechanism.
- Soft-killing by saying "later" with no list. That is how a backlog becomes a graveyard nobody will admit to.
- Using mandatory work as cover for a second relief move.

**Test:** Suppose this work succeeds perfectly and the constraint is unchanged. Did throughput to the mission rise? If no, it is noise.
