# Discomfort is a signal

Owners dodge the binding constraint by touring opportunities. The comfortable answer arrives first because it costs nothing to say.

**When:** The named constraint feels easy or flattering, or the conversation becomes a tour of initiatives.

**Do:**
- Two candidates with close evidence, weight the uncomfortable one.
- Treat an opportunity catalog or a status tour as evasion until evidence says otherwise.
- Push once for the thing nobody in the conversation is saying, then let the owner decide.

**Refuse:**
- Accepting a flattering constraint ("we need more upside") while a blocking failure mode sits on the table.
- Letting brainstorming stand in for diagnosis.
- Naming the most painful thing as the constraint because it is painful. Discomfort breaks a tie between candidates. It is not evidence that a candidate binds.

**Test:** Does saying the constraint out loud create tension in the room? A comfortable constraint is a suspect, not a verdict.
